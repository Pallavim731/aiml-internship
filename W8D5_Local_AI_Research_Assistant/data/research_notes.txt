Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence. Common AI applications include natural language processing, computer vision, recommendation systems, and robotics.

Machine Learning

Machine Learning is a subset of artificial intelligence in which systems learn patterns from data. Supervised learning uses labelled examples, while unsupervised learning identifies patterns in unlabelled data.

Digital Twins

A digital twin is a virtual representation of a physical object, system, or process. Digital twins can be used to monitor performance, simulate conditions, detect potential problems, and support decision making.

Smart Buildings

Smart buildings use connected systems and software to monitor and optimize building operations. Applications include HVAC monitoring, energy management, lighting control, occupancy analysis, and predictive maintenance.
