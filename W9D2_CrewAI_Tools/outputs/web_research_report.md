**Definition and Applications of Generative AI in Smart Buildings**

Generative AI refers to a type of artificial intelligence that can generate new data, patterns, and insights from existing data. In the context of smart buildings, Generative AI can be used to analyze data from various building systems, identify patterns and trends, and provide recommendations for energy efficiency, occupant comfort, and security.

3. **Building Automation Systems (BAS)**: Generative AI can optimize BAS by analyzing data from various sensors and systems to provide real-time insights and recommendations for energy efficiency and occupant comfort.
